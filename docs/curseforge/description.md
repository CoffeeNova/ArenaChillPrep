# ArenaChillPrep

![banner](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/1896/269/project-banner-png.png)

Arena preparation is short. Your to-do list is not.

Craft stones, conjure food and water, buff yourself, help your partner, trade the items… and try not to still be sorting bags when the gates open.

**ArenaChillPrep** makes the boring part of arena prep much easier for **Warlocks** and **Mages** in **WoW: TBC Anniversary**.

## What it does

### One-key preparation workflows

Create your own preparation routine and run it with a single keybind.

Use workflows for things such as:

*   Summoning your demon
*   Applying your buffs
*   Creating Healthstones or Soulstones
*   Conjuring food and water
*   Equipping your preparation items
*   Buffing your arena partner

You decide what your routine looks like. ArenaChillPrep simply helps you move through it without turning every prep phase into a piano solo.

> “I just wanted to make stones, not perform a raid-ready orchestral composition before every 2v2.”

![workflow](https://media.forgecdn.net/attachments/1896/263/workflow-warlock-png.png)

## Automatic item trading

Once your configured items are ready, ArenaChillPrep can open a trade with your arena partner and place the items into the trade window for you.

For example:

*   **Warlock:** Healthstones and Soulstones
*   **Mage:** Conjured food and water

You still confirm the trade yourself. The addon never sends gold, never trades outside arena preparation, and stops trying before the gates open.

## Your settings, your routine

ArenaChillPrep includes flexible settings for:

*   Custom workflows and keybinds
*   Which items to trade
*   How many items to pass
*   Arena bracket support
*   Trade timing and gate safety timing
*   Enabling or disabling automatic trading whenever you want

The addon stays quiet outside arena preparation, so it will not interfere with normal gameplay.

## Supported classes

Currently supported:

*   **Warlock** — workflows, Healthstones, Soulstones, and automatic trading
*   **Mage** — workflows, conjured food/water, and automatic trading

More class support may be added in future updates.

## Getting started

1.  Log in on a Warlock or Mage.
2.  Type `/acp` to open the settings.
3.  Configure your workflow and optional keybind.
4.  Choose the items you want to trade to your partner.
5.  Enter the arena and enjoy having fewer things to remember.

## Commands

*   `/acp` — Open settings
*   `/acp enable` — Enable the addon
*   `/acp disable` — Disable the addon
*   `/acp status` — Show current preparation and item status
*   `/acp debug` — Toggle debug messages

## Notes

*   Made for **World of Warcraft: TBC Anniversary**.
*   Works only during arena preparation.
*   Does not craft items for you — you still press your workflow key to create them.
*   Does not automatically confirm trades.
*   Does not send gold.
